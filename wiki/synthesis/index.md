# Synthesis Index

Entry point for the synthesis domain. It records the answers the vault has worked out by combining its own articles — the question asked and the pages that answered it — filed here so that an exploration compounds into the knowledge base the way an ingested source does, instead of disappearing into chat history. Written answers are listed as links; questions worth answering that do not yet have a page are listed as plain text, so the map of open questions stays visible without linking to pages that do not exist.
