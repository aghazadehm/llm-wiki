# Outcome Over Output

Outcome Over Output is the principle of focusing on the results we want to achieve (behavior changes, business impacts) rather than the features or work we produce (outputs). It shifts the question from "Did we ship it?" to "Did it achieve what we wanted?"

## Related Concepts

- [[Impact Mapping]] — operationalizes outcome-over-output by tracing deliverables to impacts and goals.
- [[Feature Injection]] — ensures every feature delivers a desired outcome.
- [[Outside-In Development]] — starts from desired outcomes and works inward.
- [[User Story Mapping]] — helps find the thinnest slice that delivers an outcome, not just builds features.

## My Understanding

Outputs are things we can count: number of features shipped, story points completed, lines of code. Outcomes are changes in behavior or state: more users complete onboarding, support calls decrease, conversion rate increases. The trap is treating outputs as the goal.

This principle is fundamental to modern product development. Teams can ship lots of features (high output) but fail to move business metrics (no outcome). Conversely, small changes can have outsized impact. 

To apply it: define success in terms of measurable outcomes upfront, validate assumptions about what causes those outcomes, and be willing to stop or pivot if delivered features don't achieve them. Techniques like impact mapping make this concrete by forcing the link between deliverables and desired behavior changes.
